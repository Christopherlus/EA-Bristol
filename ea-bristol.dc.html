What we run

Four things, all hands-on.

Pick a problem, work it with people who care about getting it right, and find out fast whether this is for you. None of it is ordered by priority; start wherever you fit.

For new members

Intro Project Program

Test the ideas with your hands, not just your highlighter.

Pick a real problem, work it with a small team, and see if this is actually for you. A short, structured project for people new to EA who want to try the ideas out rather than read about them first.

One-to-one

Mentor Program

A straight answer from someone who's actually walked the path.

Paired one-to-one with someone further along in research, policy, or industry who can tell you what a path actually looks like. Built around regular check-ins, not a one-off coffee chat.

Drop-in

Coworking Sessions

A room full of people getting real work done. Bring yours.

A standing block of time and a room full of people doing focused work. Show up, put your headphones on, get more done than you would alone.

Every 2 weeks

Biweekly Socials

Same people, no agenda, good food.

No pitch, no deadline. Every other week the same people you'd be working alongside show up for food, a drink, and a conversation that isn't about your next due date.

Why we exist

Good intentions are the starting point. We care about the strategy and the execution it takes to actually move the needle. We'd rather test an idea than just discuss it.

Mentorship

Real people, further down the path.

Every mentor here has actually done the thing; research, policy, building. They give straight answers about what a path looks like, not a pep talk.
